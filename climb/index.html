Field guide

How Oliver climbs

  • ← → or A D move. ↑ W Space jumps. Press jump again in the air — a gold ring means the double jump caught.
  • The path zigzags. Some gaps need that second jump. Pokémon Centers and flags are checkpoints: touch one, and a fall or an empty VOLT bar sends you back there, not to the bottom.
  • E or Q, or the VOLT bar, opens the Question Terminal. Keys 1–4 answer. A streak stores a bigger spark.
  • Running and jumping spend VOLT. Bounce pads are free. Higher up, platforms slide.
  • Fall off the bottom and you land back on the last ledge. The camera only climbs.
  • Five zones: Pallet Meadow, Viridian Canopy, Cerulean Falls, Zapdos Spire, Champion Summit at 1,000 m.
  • Tokens at the end of a climb buy packs. Equip any Pokémon you catch.
